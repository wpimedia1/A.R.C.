# Engineering references and their applicability

Reviewed during the November 3, 2026 repair. Published reference values below are not measurements of the hypothetical outpost. The package records which operating points are applied; unused references are not silently substituted into jobs.

| Topic | Public reference | Reported quantity | Application in this build |
|---|---|---|---|
| RedWater demand | [Honeybee Robotics, Ninth International Conference on Mars, 2019, poster 6333](https://www.hou.usra.edu/meetings/ninthmars2019/eposter/6333.pdf) | Whole-system design estimate approximately 10–15 kW | Drilling reserves the upper endpoint, 15 kW. This is a conservative allocation within that design estimate, not measured drilling-motor demand or a certified transient peak bound. Laboratory penetration and field melt extraction remain different operating points. |
| Rover mobility | [NASA Perseverance rover components](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/mars-2020-perseverance/rover-components/) | Driving consumes less than 200 W; maximum reference speed 152 m/h on flat hard ground | 200/152 Wh/m is a reference operating-point calculation. It is not a terrain-independent consumption ceiling; slower driving and housekeeping require additional characterization. |
| Rover battery | [JPL battery-system design, testing and operation presentation](https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/battery-system-design-testing-and-operation-for-the-mars-perseverance-rover-and-ingenuity-helicopter.pdf), slide 5 | 2,408 Wh at 20 °C; battery temperature limits −20 to +30 °C | Finite reference storage, mobility drain and generator replenishment. A full initial battery is explicitly an initial-condition assumption. Ambient MCD temperature is not used as internal battery temperature. |
| Electronics | [NASA RAD750 heritage presentation](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/20070032045/downloads/20070032045.pdf), PDF page 30; [NASA NIAC SPEAR report](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/20250006656/downloads/NIAC_2019_PhI_Howe_SPEAR.pdf), PDF page 15 | RAD750 temperature range −55 to +125 °C; cited total-ionizing-dose capability up to 1 Mrad | Component-specific operating-envelope experiments, with units rad(Si). Not a whole-machine guarantee. Biological dose in mSv cannot populate this limit. No local component sensor is invented. |
| Repair duration | [NASA/TM—2012-216957](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/20110020834/downloads/20110020834.pdf), printed page 66 | Defined mechanical rover repair study: 7.5 hours surface crew-or-robot work and 35 hours ground support | Research reference only. The authors warn that the estimates lack design-level fidelity. The app does not apply this procedure to RASSOR or represent a robot with unmodeled repair tooling. |
| Robotic replacement capability | [NASA modular excavation robot, 2020](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20210012148); [MICE, 2024](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20240003128) | Modular interfaces and robotic disconnect/replacement concepts and demonstrations | Module repair execution now supports compatible service equipment, diagnosed components, finite spare manifests and staged power/time. Actual compatible machinery and procedure inputs remain unconfigured; the default mission still uses complete-unit replacement. |
| Battery swapping | [NASA Lunar Mobility white paper](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/20240004101/downloads/Lunar%20Mobility%20White%20Paper%20LSIC.pdf), PDF pages 6–7; [Ocado industrial swap-unit datasheet](https://139494159.fs1.hubspotusercontent-eu1.net/hubfs/139494159/Datasheets%20Download/OIA_BatterySwapUnit_datasheet_2024_v2.pdf) | NASA discusses robot-compatible batteries; Ocado reports a terrestrial swap in under 60 seconds | Capability evidence exists. The industrial station is not Mars-qualified or a modeled RASSOR interface. Serial swap, removed-pack charging and reuse now execute for an explicit engineering configuration. No terrestrial station or timing is installed as a Mars default; the current mission still uses charging. |
| Cargo offloading | [NASA 2015 surface operations concept](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/20150010739/downloads/20150010739.pdf), slide 23; [2016 revision](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/20160009226/downloads/20160009226.pdf) | Notional 2015 cargo workflow includes 18 hours preparation, 38.25 hours for two 500 kg rovers, and 48 hours for a 3 t laboratory. The 2016 allocation differs. | These are useful payload-specific concept schedules, not observed offloads or a duration for the 90 t habitat. No silent mixing of revisions. |
| Large habitat offload | [NASA, Offloading a 90-Ton Hab from its Lander on Mars](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/20220010430/downloads/Offloading%20a%2090-Ton%20Hab%20from%20its%20Lander%20on%20Mars.pdf) | Specific heavy-payload offload concept | Staged lowering/rotation, static moment/torque/cable/braking/time checks, payload state and readiness gates are implemented. A compatible lifting/transport design remains unconfigured. Baseline D4 still assumes a preplaced habitat; it cannot demonstrate offload. |
| Arcadia environment | [Mars Climate Database access and conditions](https://www-mars.lmd.jussieu.fr/mars/access.html) | Coordinate- and season-specific modeled air/surface temperatures and pressure | Actual MCD 6.2 output is ingested, hashed, plotted and available to agents. An explicit optional thermal profile can use its pinned air-temperature series as a heat-balance boundary. It remains climatology, not internal telemetry or a measured safety bound. |

The source-verification report checks the package's quoted passages against archived documents. It does not validate cross-document design compatibility. In particular, applying a lunar reactor deployment concept, Mars rover operating point and experimental excavation design in one mission is a disclosed engineering study, not a NASA-approved integrated mission design.

SWIM consistency and radar products inform prospecting. They cannot establish site-scale ice concentration or a recoverable reserve. The finite water feed and storage in the simulator are scenario allocations, never claimed as observations.

Whole RedWater mass/capital and a validated compatible machinery/thermal design remain required for mission-level claims. Executable service/offload and coupled lumped thermal calculations are now implemented; the required hardware/calibration manifests are not fabricated. See ENGINEERING-PROCEDURES.md.

The new heat-balance and load calculations bundle actual NASA/NIST retrieval hashes in mission/physics-references.json. They do not convert these concept references into equipment certification.

A newly verified NASA Rodwell paper provides establishment prerequisites and a specific alternative configuration mass estimate. Its scope, hash, pages and remaining implementation are in [WATER-MODEL-REVIEW.md](WATER-MODEL-REVIEW.md). It is not substituted into the RedWater operating model.
