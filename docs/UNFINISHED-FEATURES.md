## Additional remaining implementation and acceptance gaps

- No physical robots, humanoids, craft interfaces or safety-qualified equipment controllers are connected. The platform currently executes software state transitions.
- Work creation supports package templates and operator-configured repair/swap/charge/offload procedures. General construction assembly, manufacturing, agriculture, autonomous physical diagnosis, inspections/rework and machine-specific flight-qualified procedures remain incomplete.
- Survey hazard analysis crosses an orbital image window; actual inter-asset/work-site routing, local terrain deformation and fine traversability are not implemented. 3D models/layouts remain illustrative and explicitly labeled. The unannotated site crop and no-repeat rendering passed rebuilt-image browser inspection; physical-model/asset fidelity remains open.
- Finite water feed/storage are design allocations; a detailed material-processing plant, losses and storage hardware qualification remain incomplete.
- Optional calibrated-by-input housekeeping/heater draw and lumped thermal conditioning are now implemented; the default rover still has no selected physical calibration profile. Terrain loads, capacity degradation and multi-node thermal behavior remain incomplete. RASSOR and reactor envelopes/failure modes are incomplete; the processor envelope is component-specific.
- The schedule combines published concepts and scenario decisions. It is not a published, integrated NASA colonization/fiscal schedule. The default preplaced-habitat study excludes offload; explicitly configuring an offload study makes its placement mandatory for shielding/readiness.
- Replacement logistics uses idealized orbital timing and historical landed-mass cost scaling; launch/landing vehicles, delivery manifests, supplier contracts and validated itemized prices are absent.
- The fault-suite delay case restores equipment availability after six steps; the same automatic replacement policy may already have ordered a unit. Cancellation/reuse of such orders is not implemented.
- Real engine-9 acceptance is recorded separately (67 provider-matched responses). Current engine-10 standing orders and four bounded real-agent fault comparisons are verified. Full readiness failed because water remained held; post-readiness execution and full-duration recovery remain unverified. No superiority claim is supported. Authenticated GET retrieval matched 51 historical records to Meta response identity, model, usage and public tool calls. One response ID returned HTTP 404; three records had no retrievable response ID. These four remain unverified. This does not validate the repaired planner through new generation; see historical-response-check.json.
- Returned provider usage now reconciles before tool-argument parsing; malformed proposals preserve identity, actual usage and an explicit invalid-proposal status. This is regression-tested with injected responses, not new live inference.
- Fork state/accounting isolation is tested. A separate audit of all dynamic weather/memory context against a strict fork evidence-time cutoff remains outstanding; not every auxiliary observation is represented as a mission source pin.
- Full deterministic event-command replay across specification migrations, long-duration restart soak, and all external-source failure/release permutations are not newly established by the regression suite.
- Browser automation covers ordinary view navigation and selected interactions. It does not establish human usability, screen-reader acceptance, NASA audio playback on the owner’s machine, or accuracy of 3D asset physics.
- Full license compatibility/redistribution review, final submission video, architecture narration recording and owner-approved publication remain unfinished. Copyright attribution is retained; public source access is not blanket reuse permission.
